# How to Prepare Your Home for an Open Home: 15 Tips to Impress Buyers

**To prepare your home for an open home, focus on five things: deep clean every room, declutter and depersonalise, tidy the street appeal, maximise natural light and fresh air, and fix small maintenance issues. None of these need expensive renovations, but together they help buyers picture themselves living in your home.**

First impressions matter. Whether you're selling a family home, townhouse or apartment, a little preparation can make a big difference to how buyers feel when they walk through the door. Here are our 15 tips.

## 1. Give your home a thorough clean

A clean home feels more inviting and well maintained. Before your open home, give every room a thorough clean, paying particular attention to:

- Kitchen benchtops, appliances and cupboards
- Bathroom sinks, showers, toilets and mirrors
- Floors, carpets and skirting boards
- Windows and glass doors
- Dust on shelves, furniture and light fittings

Don't forget the little details, like fingerprints on doors and marks on walls. Buyers notice more than you might think.

## 2. Declutter, depersonalise and create space

Removing clutter is one of the easiest ways to make your home more appealing. Clear kitchen benchtops, tidy bookshelves, organise cupboards and remove excess furniture where you can. The goal is rooms that feel spacious, open and easy to move around, so buyers focus on the home's features rather than your belongings.

## 3. Make a great first impression at the front

Street appeal sets the tone for the whole viewing, because the exterior is the first thing buyers see. Before the open home:

- Mow the lawns
- Trim hedges and garden edges
- Remove weeds
- Clean the front door and entrance
- Add fresh flowers or pot plants
- Clean paths and the driveway

## 4. Let in natural light

Bright homes feel larger, warmer and more inviting. Open all curtains and blinds, clean the windows to let more light through, and add lamps to any naturally darker rooms.

## 5. Create a fresh, welcoming smell

Smell has a big impact on how buyers experience a home. Open windows to let fresh air in, empty the bins, clean the kitchen and bathrooms, deal with pet odours and avoid strong cooking smells beforehand. Aim for fresh and neutral rather than overpowering air fresheners.

## 6. Style your kitchen

The kitchen is often one of the most important rooms for buyers. Keep benchtops clear, put dishes away and store unnecessary appliances. A bowl of fresh fruit, a small vase of flowers, polished surfaces and a spotless sink help buyers appreciate the space and how it works.

## 7. Make your bathrooms shine

Bathrooms should feel fresh, clean and well maintained. Clean mirrors, taps and shower screens, put away personal toiletries, swap worn towels for fresh ones, close toilet lids and make sure there's no mould or unpleasant smell. Fresh white towels and a tidy vanity create a clean, hotel-like feel.

## 8. Tidy bedrooms and living areas

Bedrooms and living areas should feel relaxing and spacious. Make the beds, arrange cushions neatly, clear bedside tables and keep floors free so buyers can move easily around each room and imagine how they'd use the space.

## 9. Present your outdoor living spaces

Decks, patios, balconies and gardens can be major selling points. Clean outdoor furniture, sweep decks and patios, tidy garden beds, remove cobwebs, clean outdoor windows and doors, and add a few cushions. Help buyers imagine their morning coffee or a summer barbecue with friends.

## 10. Take care of small repairs

Small maintenance issues can make buyers wonder what else hasn't been looked after. Before the open home, fix loose door handles, dripping taps, blown light bulbs, squeaky doors, damaged cupboard handles and minor wall marks. These quick jobs help present your property as cared for and ready for its next owner.

## 11. Set a comfortable temperature

Temperature affects how long buyers stay. On colder days, warm the home before the open home starts; in warmer weather, let fresh air circulate. Buyers who feel comfortable take their time.

## 12. Put away personal items

Buyers need to imagine themselves living in your home. Put away excess family photos, personal paperwork, valuables and important documents, bathroom toiletries, and pet accessories. You don't need to remove all personality, but a neutral, tidy presentation appeals to more buyers.

## 13. Make your home safe and easy to move through

Clear hallways, stairs and entrances, secure pets, remove trip hazards and make sure outdoor paths are accessible. Store valuables, medications and personal documents safely out of sight.

## 14. Add a few finishing touches

Small details make a home feel special: fresh flowers in the living area, neatly arranged cushions, fresh linen on the beds, a simple dining table setting, healthy pot plants and coordinated towels. Avoid over-styling. The aim is natural, inviting and easy to imagine living in. For more ideas, see our [simple home styling tips](/blog/home-styling-tips-simple-ways-to-make-your-home-look-its-best/).

## 15. Leave the property during the open home

It's generally best for sellers to leave while the agent runs the open home. Buyers explore more freely, talk openly about the property and take their time looking around. Just make sure your agent has everything they need before you go.

## Frequently asked questions about open homes

### How long does it take to prepare a house for an open home?
It depends on your home, so start early. Tackle decluttering and small repairs first, then finish with a thorough clean, a garden tidy and the finishing touches just before buyers arrive.

### Should I be home during an open home?
It's usually best not to be. Buyers feel more comfortable exploring, opening cupboards and discussing the property when the owner isn't there. Your agent will host the open home and share feedback with you afterwards.

### Do I need to stage my home professionally to sell?
Not necessarily. Many homes present very well with a deep clean, decluttering, good lighting and a few simple styling touches. Your agent can advise whether professional styling is worthwhile for your property and price point.

### What do buyers notice first at an open home?
Buyers notice street appeal, cleanliness, light and smell first. A tidy entrance, bright rooms and a fresh-smelling home create a positive first impression before buyers look at anything else.

## Preparation makes a difference

Getting ready for an open home doesn't require expensive renovations. It's the simple things that make the biggest difference: a clean home, tidy gardens, natural light, fresh presentation and attention to detail. Remember, you're not just selling a property; you're helping buyers imagine their future home.
