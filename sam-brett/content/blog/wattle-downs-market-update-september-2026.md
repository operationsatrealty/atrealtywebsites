# Wattle Downs Market Update – September 2026

Wattle Downs continues to be one of South Auckland's most desirable suburbs, and September's figures show why. Whether you're considering selling or looking to invest, here's what's happening on the ground.

## Strong Demand, Limited Stock

Wattle Downs buyers remain highly motivated. We're seeing properties move quickly, with multiple offers common on well-presented homes. The current inventory sits at a healthy level, but premium properties (3+ bed, modern kitchen, good sections) are snapped up within weeks.

**For sellers:** Now is an excellent time to list. Buyer demand is strong, and you have room to negotiate premium pricing.

**For buyers:** Be prepared to act fast. Have your finances in order and be ready to move decisively when you find the right property.

## Price Trends

Median selling prices in Wattle Downs continue to appreciate steadily. We're seeing:
- **3-bed homes**: $850k–$950k (up 3% year-on-year)
- **4-bed homes**: $1.0m–$1.2m
- **Sections**: Premium areas commanding $550k+ for a bare section

Homes with modern kitchens, open-plan living, and good outdoor space command top dollar.

## What Buyers Want

From our conversations with buyers, the most sought-after features are:
1. **Location**: Proximity to schools, parks, and transport
2. **Modern finishes**: Updated kitchens and bathrooms
3. **Outdoor space**: Good-sized sections with potential
4. **Parking**: Double garage or off-street parking

## Selling Tips

If you're thinking of listing:
- **Get it market-ready**: A fresh coat of paint, tidy landscaping, and clean interiors make a huge difference
- **Price strategically**: We can help you find the sweet spot between maximising value and attracting buyers
- **Professional photos**: Quality marketing matters in this market
- **Be flexible**: Showing availability for viewings speeds up the process
