# South Auckland Property Market Update — September 2026

The latest REINZ figures paint a nuanced picture of the South Auckland property market. While the national median price sits at $750,000 (down 1.3% year-on-year) and Auckland's broader median has softened to $950,000, South Auckland suburbs are tracking their own path — and the data is more encouraging than many expected.

## Wattle Downs: Outperforming the Region

Wattle Downs continues to be one of South Auckland's standout performers. REINZ data shows the suburb's median sale price over the past 12 months at **$985,000**, representing a **6.5% increase** compared to the previous year. With 121 properties changing hands in that period, buyer demand remains consistent.

The average house value in Wattle Downs as at March 2026 sits at $957,050, and recent sales are regularly achieving prices in the high-$900,000 to low-$1.1 million range — particularly for well-presented four-bedroom homes on larger sections.

What's driving Wattle Downs? The suburb benefits from its established streetscapes, proximity to the Manukau Harbour, quality schooling options, and relatively generous section sizes compared to newer developments.

## Manurewa: Steady and Accessible

Manurewa's median sale price in April 2026 came in at **$747,500**, down a modest 2.2% year-on-year. Over a three-year window, Manurewa has declined just 3.3% — far less than the double-digit drops some Auckland suburbs experienced during the post-2021 correction.

For buyers, Manurewa represents one of the most accessible entry points in the Auckland market, with prices well below the $950,000 regional median. The suburb's ongoing town-centre revitalisation and improving transport links add long-term appeal.

## Papakura: A Buyer's Market

Papakura's median has pulled back to **$754,500**, down 5.7% year-on-year. The House Price Index for the district sits at 3,603, reflecting a softer stretch — though prices remain well above 2019 levels. For buyers prepared to take a medium-term view, Papakura's current pricing relative to its infrastructure (motorway access, train station, town centre amenities) presents genuine value.

## What This Means for Sellers

If you own a property in Wattle Downs or the surrounding suburbs, the data suggests the market is rewarding well-presented homes with competitive pricing. Properties that are realistically priced and properly marketed are still achieving strong results.

The key factors driving sale prices in the current market:

- **Presentation matters more than ever.** Buyers have more choice, so first impressions count.
- **Pricing accuracy is critical.** Overpriced listings are sitting longer, and the longer a property sits, the weaker the eventual sale price tends to be.
- **Marketing reach counts.** With sales volumes down 13% nationally compared to August 2025, the pool of active buyers is smaller — your marketing needs to find them wherever they are.

## What This Means for Buyers

South Auckland remains one of the best-value markets in Auckland. With the national median at $750,000 and Manurewa sitting just below that, buyers here get more house for their dollar than almost anywhere else in the region.

Current support available for first-home buyers includes:

- **KiwiSaver first-home withdrawal** — access your KiwiSaver balance (less the $1,000 minimum) toward your deposit
- **Kāinga Ora First Home Loan** — purchase with as little as 5% deposit instead of the standard 20%
- **RBNZ new-build LVR exemption** — new builds can qualify for lower deposit requirements

The First Home Grant (formerly HomeStart) was discontinued in May 2024, but these remaining pathways still make homeownership achievable at South Auckland price points.

## Looking Ahead

The South Auckland market has shown resilience through 2026. Interest rate movements and any changes to lending restrictions will be the key factors to watch through the final quarter of the year. For now, the fundamentals — population growth, constrained land supply, and relative affordability — continue to support property values in this part of Auckland.
