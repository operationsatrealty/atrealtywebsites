# Home Styling Tips: 7 Simple Ways to Make Your Home Look Its Best

**The simplest ways to style your home are to declutter, maximise natural light, use a neutral colour palette, add plants and fresh flowers, arrange furniture to open up the space, finish with small details like fresh towels and cushions, and present your outdoor areas as an extension of the home.** None of these require expensive furniture or renovations.

Whether you're preparing to sell or simply want to refresh your living space, good styling makes a big difference. Here are seven easy tips to bring out the best in your home.

## 1. Declutter and keep things simple

Less is more. Remove unnecessary items, clear benchtops and organise shelves to create a clean, spacious feel. A clutter-free home lets its best features stand out.

## 2. Make the most of natural light

Natural light makes a room feel bigger, brighter and more inviting. Open curtains and blinds, clean your windows and use mirrors to reflect light around the room.

## 3. Choose a neutral colour palette

Neutral tones such as white, beige, soft grey and warm earthy colours create a timeless, calming atmosphere that appeals to most people. Add colour through cushions, artwork, rugs and accessories, which are easy to change.

## 4. Add greenery and fresh flowers

Plants and fresh flowers are an easy, affordable way to bring life into a home. A few well-placed indoor plants add warmth, texture and a natural touch to living spaces.

## 5. Style your living spaces

Create comfortable, inviting spaces with neatly arranged cushions, soft throws and a few carefully chosen décor pieces. Position furniture to make the most of the space and allow easy movement through the room.

## 6. Don't forget the finishing touches

Small details bring everything together: fresh towels in the bathroom, neatly made beds, warm lighting and a simply styled dining table. Aim for a home that feels comfortable, welcoming and ready to enjoy.

## 7. Present your outdoor space

Outdoor areas are an extension of your home, especially through a New Zealand summer. Tidy gardens, clean outdoor furniture, fresh pot plants and a well-presented entertaining area create an inviting atmosphere.

## Does home styling help sell a house?

Yes. Styling helps buyers picture themselves living in a home, highlights its best features and makes rooms look more spacious in photos and at open homes. Most homes can be styled effectively with what you already own plus a few inexpensive touches. ## Frequently asked questions about home styling

### How can I style my home on a budget?
Start with free changes: declutter, rearrange furniture, open up the light and deep clean. Then add low-cost touches such as fresh flowers, indoor plants, new cushion covers and fresh white towels.

### What colours make a home look bigger?
Light neutral colours such as white, soft grey and warm beige reflect more light and make rooms feel larger and calmer. Keep bold colours for small, easily changed accessories.

### Should I style my home before selling?
It's worth it. A well-styled home photographs better, which attracts more buyers online, and it feels more welcoming at open homes. Your agent can advise on what will make the biggest difference for your property.

## Styling done well doesn't need to be expensive

Great home styling doesn't need expensive furniture or major renovations. With a little creativity and attention to detail, you can make your space feel fresh, welcoming and beautifully presented, whether you're refreshing it for yourself or preparing it for sale.
