Good questions

Things sellers ask me every week.

Why do you push auctions so hard?

Because a firm date changes buyer behaviour. An auction campaign compresses interest into three weeks, puts qualified buyers in one room, and when the hammer falls the sale is unconditional — no finance clauses, no cold feet. Four of my current nine listings are auction campaigns for exactly that reason. When an auction isn't the right fit for your property or your timeline, I'll tell you that too.

What does an appraisal involve, and what does it cost?

Nothing — it's free and there's no obligation. I walk the property, pull the recent comparable sales around you, and give you an honest range plus a recommended campaign. You'll leave the conversation knowing what your home is worth and what I'd do to sell it, whether or not you list with me.

My property is a small unit, not a showpiece home. Will it get real attention?

Look at my current listings: alongside a five-bedroom home in Goodwood Heights I'm running campaigns for one-bedroom units at $290,000 and $489,000. Every campaign gets professional marketing and a proper strategy. A big part of my 77 sales in the last 24 months has been exactly this kind of property.

How will I know what's happening during my campaign?

You'll hear from me after every open home and every significant buyer conversation — my reviews say "at all sorts of hours" and that's a habit, not an accident. You will never be the last to know anything about your own sale.
