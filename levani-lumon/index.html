About Levani

Calm, methodical, and proven when it matters most.

Levani Lum-On is the agent vendors turn to when a sale is complicated — and equally the one first-home buyers and long-time family owners trust for a straightforward, well-guided result. With over 20 years of experience across South, Central and West Auckland, his real strength is range.

Mortgagee and distressed sales. Investment, development and land banking. KiwiSaver first-home purchases. Families selling the home they raised their kids in. Levani brings the same calm, methodical approach to every one of them — which is why much of his business now comes from repeat clients and referrals, with 223 confirmed career sales worth more than $157 million.

A Ray White Executive Performer and a past president of the Rotary Club of Papatoetoe Central, Levani is deeply rooted in the South Auckland community he serves — based in Manukau and active across Papatoetoe, Manurewa, Māngere, Ōtara, Mt Wellington, Mount Roskill, Te Atatu Peninsula and Massey.
