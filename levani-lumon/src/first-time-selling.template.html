First time selling

Never sold a home before? Here's exactly how it goes.

Selling your first home is a bigger decision than buying it was — and nobody hands you a manual. This is the whole process, in order, in plain English, so you know what happens and when before you commit to anything.

1. The free appraisal

I walk through your home, compare it against recent sales around you, and give you an honest value range — not an inflated number to win your listing. It costs nothing, and there's no obligation. You'll leave this conversation knowing what your home is worth and what I'd do to sell it.

2. Choosing how to sell

Auction, advertised price, by negotiation, deadline sale — each method suits different homes and markets. I'll recommend one and explain exactly why, including what it costs and what the risks are. The choice stays yours.

3. Getting the home ready

Small things move buyers more than big renovations: decluttering, gardens, light. I'll tell you what's worth doing and — just as important — what isn't worth spending money on before we photograph and launch.

4. The campaign

Professional photography, portal listings, my buyer database, open homes. You'll hear from me after every open home and every significant buyer conversation — you will never be the last to know anything about your own sale.

5. Offers, negotiation, or auction day

Whether it's bids in a room or offers on paper, this is where experience earns its keep. I handle the pressure and the negotiation; you make the decisions with full information. Nothing is accepted without your say-so.

6. Unconditional to settlement

Once the sale goes unconditional it's real. Your lawyer handles the paperwork, I keep everything moving, and settlement day is when the keys change hands and the money lands. I stay reachable the whole way — and for years after, most of my business comes from exactly these sellers.
