Off-market properties

Sold quietly, without the open homes.

An off-market sale is a property sold without public advertising — no portal listing, no signboard, no open homes. After twenty years in this market I hold a deep database of qualified buyers, and some of my sales happen entirely inside it.

When off-market is the right call

Off-market suits sellers who value privacy over publicity: a family situation you'd rather keep off the internet, a tenanted investment where open homes are hard, a complicated circumstance that benefits from discretion, or simply testing the market at your price before committing to a campaign.

It's also how buyers get first access. The buyers in my database hear about these properties before anyone else does — often the only time they hear about them at all.

The honest trade-off

I'll always tell you this straight: a public campaign creates competition, and competition is usually what produces the best price. Off-market trades some of that competitive tension for speed, privacy and certainty. For some properties and some situations that trade is exactly right — and when it isn't, I'll recommend the open market instead. Which path fits is a conversation, not a default.

Selling quietly, or buying early

Selling? Your property goes to matched, qualified buyers from my database only. No photography day, no signboard, nothing public unless and until you choose otherwise.

Buying? Register what you're looking for and you'll hear about suitable off-market properties before they ever reach the portals.
