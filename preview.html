About Tom

From the rugby field to your front door.

Before real estate, I spent 15 years as a professional rugby player — eight of them representing Auckland and the Blues, before heading to Ireland with Connacht and embracing the European lifestyle. That career taught me discipline, teamwork and how to perform when it matters most.

Today I channel that same drive into property. With more than half a decade in the industry and over $100 million in settled sales, I work out of Ray White Manurewa — part of the award-winning A T Realty group across South & Central Auckland.

I bought my first home in East Auckland at just 22, and I've been an active South Auckland investor ever since. As a hands-on builder who manages his own tenants, I understand yield, multi-income properties, land banking and development potential — so whether you're selling the family home or building a portfolio, you're in experienced hands.
